## Two versions of the email — pick one

**`メール_HTML版.html` — the designed one.** Laid out like the hub: the dark
tank-water palette, the cyan accent, the official Fujie on the cover, the real
sturgeon photo full width, a shot of the timeline, and four small pictures of
the chibi, the plush, the caviar tin and the pin set. Both links are buttons.

**The plain text below — the safe one.** Same words, no design.

### How to send the designed one

Gmail's compose box will not accept HTML pasted as code. Do this instead:

1. Double-click `メール_HTML版.html` so it opens in Chrome.
2. Click once on the page, then press **Ctrl+A** then **Ctrl+C**.
3. In Gmail, start a new message and press **Ctrl+V** in the body.

The layout, colours and pictures come across. Send yourself a test first and
open it on your phone before sending the real one.

### What to know before choosing

- **Pictures are off by default.** Gmail hides images from senders you have not
  emailed before, so the first thing the judges see may be the email with every
  picture missing. It still reads correctly — every word, both links and both
  buttons are live text, not pictures — but the impact is lost until they click
  "images を表示". The plain text version has no such gap.
- **The pictures are loaded from the live site**, not attached, so the email
  itself stays small and will not clip.
- **A designed email is slightly likelier to land in Promotions or be held by a
  corporate filter** than a plain one. With the deadline on 30 September, that
  is the one real risk. If you send the designed version, send it early enough
  that you would notice no acknowledgement within three working days.

If you want the safest possible delivery, send the plain text. If you want the
entry to look like the work, send the designed one — it is a creative contest,
and the judges are the PR office.

## Note on the artwork

The official Fujie illustration is used **unmodified** throughout, exactly as the
character manual (ver 1.0, Article 7) requires — no reshaping, recolouring,
transparency, overlaid text, design changes, or added/removed elements.

「ちびフジィ」 is presented as a **proposed new second form** for a future
manual ver 2.0 — not as an alteration of the existing character. This distinction
is made explicitly on slide 9 of the deck.
