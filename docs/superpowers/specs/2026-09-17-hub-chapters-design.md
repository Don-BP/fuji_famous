# Hub restructure: chapters, landing grid, five new ideas

Date: 2026-09-17. Agreed in conversation; this is the note to build from.

## Why
The hub had thirteen ideas in a row at equal weight and no shape. The brief
asks for ideas beyond goods; the page should read that way.

## Page order
hero → landing grid → 01 この魚 → 02 二つの姿 → 03 施策 → 04 受け皿 → 05 2027年の、ある一日 → footer

## Chapters
1. この魚 — the fish section (Nishibori, 5%, ten thousand at Satomi).
2. 二つの姿 — official Fujie and Chibi Fujie; the stickers.
3. 施策 — game, timeline, voice, packaging, Aqua World tank, 100-year letter,
   manhole covers, open day, チョウザメの日.
4. 受け皿 — goods (five lines), plush, patterns. One preview row each.
5. 2027 — the four scenes, last.

## Landing grid
Under the hero. One tile per idea, grouped under the five chapter headings.
Tile = small image + title; click scrolls to the idea on the same page.

## Chapter bar
Top nav shows the five chapter names and a thin progress line; the current
chapter highlights on scroll. On phones: progress line + current chapter name.

## Idea template
Every idea: number, chapter tag, title, one paragraph, one image (goods lines
keep a one-row gallery), the case button. New ideas get a placeholder frame
until images are generated.

## Five new cases (settled)
- Aqua World Ōarai: a live sturgeon tank beside the shark tank, "if the aquarium agrees"; a 2027 special exhibition as where it could go.
- 100-year letter: children who visit write to "the 2027 class" (the year-group, not one fish; no tags needed), sealed to the 100th anniversary in 2030. Adopt-a-fish folded in.
- Manhole covers: three to five across Hitachiota (farm, station, city hall). Chibi Fujie and official Fujie; official covers need manual sign-off on exact colour.
- Open day: local families, once a year at Satomi, lottery; letters in the morning, first caviar in the afternoon.
- チョウザメの日: register with 日本記念日協会 on Fujie's birthday; the open day falls on it; international side later.

## Language
Two languages throughout, including the new cases. English linted with SlopMonster before shipping.
